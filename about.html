About RateMyTeacher

Welcome to RateMyTeacher! This program enables you to get information about teachers such as how they grade, their personality, and the difficulty of each class.

Instead of the teachers telling you, students can anonymously write reviews about their experience in a class, including the grade they received. This helps students learn more about a class and hear from other students who have experienced it.

You can search for a teacher and use information such as their state, district, school, grade, and subject to find the teacher you're looking for.

If a teacher isn't listed yet, you can request that they be added to RateMyTeacher.
